# Example durability research project

## Objective

Test whether programmable rewards can align manufacturer revenue with verified product durability.

## Actors

- Current product owner
- Manufacturer
- Protocol
- Public reviewers

## Product lifecycle

Purchase, activation, continued service, ownership transfer, and failure reporting.

## Ownership transfer

The current owner may transfer the product record to another verified owner.

## Incentive model

Rewards accrue while the product remains in verified service.

## Assumptions

Device and human attestations can be disputed and audited.

## Invariants

The customer owns the product outright. Documentation does not create executable behaviour.

## Proposed contract surface

No executable actions are published by this metadata-only example.

## Evidence

Evidence must be cited and separated from assumptions.

## Known attack vectors

Self-purchases, Sybil identities, false reports, unreported failures, and duplicated identifiers.

## Unresolved questions

How should disputes, reward funding, and product disposal be governed?

No decisions have been accepted.
